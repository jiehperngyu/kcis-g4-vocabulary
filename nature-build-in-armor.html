Nature's Build-In Armor

Reading-短文

← 回首頁

Every wild animal relies on a unique survival system to stay alive. These body parts and habits help creatures navigate challenging natural environments. Consider how a river otter glides smoothly through cold water while hunting for food. The curved contour of its body cuts through currents with very little effort.

To endure freezing rivers, the otter relies on special adaptations. Its skin is covered by tough, thick fur that resists wear and tear from rough riverbanks. Beneath those guard hairs rests a fluffy layer of underfur that traps warm air. Together, these fur layers completely waterproof against the icy stream.

Other animals rely on distinct physical features to protect themselves and build homes. A threatened porcupine will raise the bristles of its sharp quills to frighten away predators. Meanwhile, birds search for soft plant material to weave sturdy nests for their eggs. Every unique trait in nature serves a clear purpose for survival.
